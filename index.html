Funding without the friction

Your business changes.
Your funding should too.

Traditional finance can be rigid. UK Merchant Capital is designed to give established businesses access to capital while keeping repayment linked to trading performance.

You agree the funding terms up front, then repay through a percentage of eligible daily card sales that you choose, subject to your total funding amount. When sales are stronger, repayments can be higher; when trading is quieter, they can be lower.

Still have a question? Speak to the team and we’ll explain the terms relevant to your business.

Speak to us ↗
How much can I apply for?+

Funding is available from £5,000 to £1m. Your total funding amount depends on your monthly cash flow and the financial health of your business, and is subject to eligibility, assessment and approval.

How are repayments calculated?+

You choose the percentage of your eligible daily card sales that goes towards repayments, subject to your total funding amount. Because repayments are linked to daily card sales, on quieter days you repay less and on busier days you repay more.

How quickly can funds be available?+

Funding can be available quickly once an application has been assessed and approved. Timing depends on your application and the required checks.

What can I use the funding for?+

Businesses may use funding for purposes such as working capital, expansion, stock, equipment, marketing and other business needs, subject to the agreed terms.

Is this the same as a traditional business loan?+

The repayment structure is different from a conventional fixed-term loan because repayments are linked to eligible card sales. We’ll explain the exact structure and cost before you decide whether to proceed.

Will applying affect my credit score?+

Requesting a call is free and there’s no obligation to proceed. We’ll explain any checks that may be required, and when, before they happen.
